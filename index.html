About

A cozy corner of Puerto Rico on Colonial Drive

The Factory Bakery is a Puerto Rican bakery and Latin restaurant serving mofongo, pernil, sandwiches, pastries and traditional Latin dishes — with dine-in, takeout and delivery. Reviewers describe a cozy spot known for excellent service, flavorful food and a staff that makes customers feel like family, with dishes prepared quickly and with great care.

"The best in Florida — same flavor to Puerto Rico."
